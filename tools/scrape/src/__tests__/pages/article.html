Harbour project clears final review

By the transport desk

The council approved the harbour scheme on Tuesday after a session that ran past midnight, ending a review that had taken three years and two public consultations.

We have listened, and we have changed the design twice.

What we have now is something the whole city can use.

Work is expected to begin in the spring. The full decision is published on the council's site.
